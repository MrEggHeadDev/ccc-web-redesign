About Us

Minds for truth, hearts for love, hands for service.

As a private college-preparatory school in the Diocese of St. Petersburg, Clearwater Central Catholic High School is committed to educating our students with the ideals of “minds for truth, hearts for love, and hands for service.”

Our distinctive and focused academic program inspires students to fully develop their moral, physical, spiritual, intellectual, and creative potential, as well as learn to respect diversity in the world around them.
